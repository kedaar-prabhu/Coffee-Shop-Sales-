# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

A data-driven retail sales analysis project aimed at uncovering actionable business insights to optimize store operations, peak staffing, inventory management, and revenue growth.

---

## 📑 Project Overview
The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales transaction data from a coffee shop chain operating across three locations to gain actionable insights that enhance business performance, improve customer experience, and boost overall revenue.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary
* **Total Revenue:** $698,812.33
* **Total Items Sold:** 214,470 units
* **Total Transactions (Footfall):** 149,116
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $4.69
* **Average Item Unit Price:** $3.38
* **Analysis Period:** January 2023 – June 2023 (6 Months)

---

## 🎯 Key Questions & Findings

### 1. How do sales vary by day of the week and hour of the day? Are there any peak times for sales activity?

* **Peak Hours:**
  * **Primary Peak Window:** **8:00 AM – 10:59 AM** accounts for nearly **37%** of total daily transaction volume and revenue.
  * **Top Peak Hour:** **10:00 AM** leads all hours with **26,713 items sold** ($88,673.39 revenue), followed closely by 9:00 AM ($85,169.53) and 8:00 AM ($82,699.87).
  * **Off-Peak Window:** Sales level off in the afternoon (~$40,000/hour) and drop significantly after 6:00 PM.
* **Day of the Week:**
  * Sales are steadily distributed throughout the week.
  * **Busiest Days:** **Friday** ($101,373.00 | 21,701 transactions) and **Thursday** ($100,767.78 | 21,654 transactions).
  * **Slowest Days:** **Saturday** ($96,894.48 | 20,510 transactions) and **Sunday** ($98,330.31 | 21,096 transactions).

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Optimize Peak Hour Staffing:** Increase barista shifts during **8:00 AM – 11:00 AM** to improve order fulfillment speed and reduce customer wait times.
2. **Drive Average Order Value (AOV):** With an average order spend of **$4.69** (~1.4 items/order), launch bundled deals (e.g., Coffee + Bakery pairing) to boost cross-selling.
3. **Targeted Inventory Planning:** Prioritize stock replenishment for **Ethiopia**, **Barista Espresso**, and **Brewed Chai Tea** to ensure top revenue generators never go out of stock.
